QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked

What's included in a "maintenance visit"?

Each visit covers a full safety inspection, fluid checks and top-offs, filter inspection, lubrication of moving parts, and a written service report.

What if I need a repair between scheduled visits?

Plan members get priority scheduling for repair calls. You'll be moved to the front of the line, and parts/labor are discounted at your plan rate.

Can I add more forklifts to my plan?

Absolutely. We'll simply add the additional units at the per-unit plan rate. Fleet discounts are available — ask us about it.

What happens at the end of my plan?

We'll reach out before your plan expires to discuss renewal. You can upgrade, switch plans, or go month-to-month at standard rates.

Do you service electric forklifts?

Yes — we service electric, LPG, diesel, and gasoline forklifts. Battery and charging system service is included.

Is there a contract?

Yes, both plans are on a service agreement. The 6-month and 12-month commitments are what allow us to offer discounted flat rates.
