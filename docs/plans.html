Your 90-Day Checkup

No matter which plan you choose, every 90 days our technician comes to your facility for a full scheduled maintenance visit.

01
Fluid Service

Oil, hydraulic fluid, and coolant checked, topped off, or changed as needed.

02
Filter & Belts

Air, oil, and fuel filters inspected. Belts and chains adjusted or replaced.

03
Safety Check

04
Service Report

Written report of everything checked, work performed, and any issues flagged.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked

What's included in every 90-day checkup?

Fluid checks and top-offs, filter inspection, belt and chain adjustment, full safety system testing, lubrication of all moving parts, and a written service report.

What if I need a repair between checkups?

Plan members get priority scheduling. We'll get a tech out fast, and your plan rate applies to parts and labor.

Can I add more forklifts to my plan?

Yes — additional units can be added at any time at the per-unit plan rate. Fleet discounts available, just ask.

How does monthly billing work?

You'll be billed the same amount every month for the duration of your plan term. No surprise charges.

What happens when my plan ends?

We'll reach out before it expires to discuss renewal. You can upgrade, switch plans, or continue month-to-month.

How do I get pricing?

Pricing is custom based on your fleet size, equipment type, and location. Email us at info@apexliftsolutionsusa.com and we'll get you a quote fast.

Ready to Lock In Your Rate?
