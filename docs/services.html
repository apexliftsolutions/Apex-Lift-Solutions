WHAT WE DO

Our Services

From emergency breakdowns to scheduled preventive maintenance — Apex Lift Solutions has you covered.

SERVICE 01

Diagnostic & Repair

When your forklift goes down, every minute matters. Our technicians perform thorough diagnostics to identify root causes fast — no guesswork, no unnecessary parts replacements.

  • Hydraulic system diagnosis and repair
  • Electrical fault detection and rewiring
  • Engine and transmission repair
  • Mast, carriage, and fork inspection/repair
  • Battery and charging system service (electric forklifts)
  • Brake and tire replacement
SERVICE 02

Preventive Maintenance

The best repair is the one you never need. Our scheduled preventive maintenance keeps your forklifts running at peak performance and catches small issues before they become costly breakdowns.

  • Fluid checks and changes (oil, hydraulic, coolant)
  • Filter replacements
  • Belt and chain inspection and adjustment
  • Safety system testing (horns, lights, backup alarms)
  • Lubrication of all moving parts
  • Full operational safety checklist

SERVICE 03

On-Site Service Calls

We come to you. Our mobile technicians are equipped to handle most repairs and all preventive maintenance right at your facility — keeping downtime to a minimum and your team on task.

  • Mobile repair units fully stocked with common parts
  • Service at your warehouse, job site, or facility
  • Flexible scheduling around your operation hours
  • Emergency response calls available
